Pour Hour - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Pour Hour ("the game") is published by Samatxy Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data is processed when you play, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you by name. The game has no account, no sign-in, no chat and no online leaderboard. Your progress (shift, tips, rank, settings and your privacy choice) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. To understand how the game is played and to improve it, we use an analytics service, described in section 3.

2. Advertising

The game is free and shows adverts (rewarded videos you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen adverts between shifts). Adverts are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (Unity Ads Mediation) and its advertising partners, including Unity Ads. To deliver and measure adverts, and to prevent fraud, these partners may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (for example the Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or city level);
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and network type;
  • information about the adverts shown to you and how you interact with them;
  • diagnostic information and crash logs from the advertising software.

This data is collected by the advertising partners under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (LevelPlay and Unity Ads): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. Game analytics

We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics Ltd) to measure how the game is played, so that we can fix problems and make it better. Through its software in the game, GameAnalytics collects and processes on our behalf:

  • a randomly generated identifier for your installation of the game, and device identifiers provided by your operating system for apps from the same developer;
  • your IP address, used to work out your country, and device information such as model, operating system version, language and connection type;
  • how you play: when and for how long you play, which screens you open, which shifts you start, finish or fail, what happens to the drinks you pour, the in-game tips you earn and spend, and which buttons you press;
  • which adverts were offered and shown to you in the game and whether you watched them to the end, and the estimated revenue of an advert;
  • technical information: frame rate, errors and crash reports.

We see this information as statistics about all players. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not use it for advertising. GameAnalytics' privacy policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

4. Your choices

  • In the game: the first time you play, the game asks whether adverts may be personalised. You can change your answer at any time in Options > Privacy. If you decline, you still see adverts, but they are not based on your interests, and we tell our advertising partners not to sell or share your data.
  • On your device: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.

5. Legal basis and your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the game's options. Non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention, advert measurement and game analytics are based on legitimate interest. Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its processing, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best sent to the advertising partner, and requests about analytics data can be sent to us or to GameAnalytics; you can also contact us and we will help you direct your request.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children. It is intended for players aged 13 and over (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us.

7. Data retention and transfers

Game progress stays on your device. Data collected by advertising partners and by GameAnalytics is kept and may be transferred internationally according to their policies linked above.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
